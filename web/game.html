How to play
OnlineMain menu → Multiplayer. Everyone with that screen open in the same room is listed; pick a player to challenge them. Send a friend the room link to play privately.
ControllerStick or d-pad moves · X high punch · A low punch · Y high kick · B low kick · RB block · LB/RT run · LT special · Start pause · Back moves list. In menus the stick moves a pointer and A taps.
KeyboardW A S D or arrows · U high punch · I low punch · O block · J high kick · K low kick · L run · H special · P pause · M moves list.
Mouse / touchThe game's own touch controls; the mouse is a finger.
